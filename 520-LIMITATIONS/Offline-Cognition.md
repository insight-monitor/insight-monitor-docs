# Offline Cognition Blindspot

The system relies on visible digital signals. Significant knowledge work happens outside this view:

- Reading and comprehending
- Thinking and reasoning
- Comparing documents mentally
- Verbal discussions (in-person or phone)
- Sketching on paper or whiteboards
- Stepping away to reflect

## Related risks
- [[Offline-Cognition-Misclassification]] — High-cognition, low-screen-activity users may appear idle or unproductive.
