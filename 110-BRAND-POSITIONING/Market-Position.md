# Market Position

## Target markets

### Primary: Organizations with digital workflows
Enterprises and teams where work is primarily screen-based: software engineering, data analysis, customer support, operations. Buyers are team leads, project managers, and compliance officers who need nuanced visibility beyond binary monitoring.

### Secondary: Individual professionals
Freelancers, researchers, and knowledge workers who want personal productivity analytics without employer surveillance. Full data control remains with the individual.

## Differentiation

### vs. Binary activity monitors (Time Doctor, Hubstaff, Teramind)
These tools classify by application or domain name. Insight Monitor adds contextual inference — the same application can be work, learning, or distraction depending on what the user is doing within it.

### vs. Screen recording tools
Insight Monitor does not store raw screenshots for human review. All visual data passes through an inference model that extracts structured behavioral signals. Raw pixel data is not persisted.

### vs. Blocking/restriction tools
Insight Monitor does not block, restrict, or enforce. It observes, infers, and reports. Enforcement decisions belong to the organization, not the monitoring layer.

### vs. AI productivity trackers (Rewatch, Attention Insight)
These focus on meeting analysis or narrow domains. Insight Monitor covers the full range of computer-based knowledge work with a general-purpose inference pipeline.

## What we are not
- We are not a keylogger. Keystroke content is never captured.
- We are not a surveillance tool in the covert sense. Monitoring is transparent by design.
- We are not an enforcement platform. We provide evidence, not verdicts.
- We are not a replacement for human management. Inferences are inputs, not decisions.
